# External audit brief: AMOS v0.3

Audit the v0.3 package as a falsifiable engineering experiment. In particular verify that the Compatibility Registry makes variant D operationally distinct from C, that schema topology is no longer fixed by an obsolete public contract, and that the preregistered protocol prevents post-hoc success claims.

Assess closest prior art, validity of CR-01 through CR-05, missing compatibility rules, benchmark design, statistical power and limits of any conclusion. Claims must remain domain-specific until replicated across the Site Auditor and GNSS fixtures.
